WHAT THE NAME BUYS YOU
  It does not change when your address does. Without a key your name comes
  from a rotating pseudonym and is gone by tomorrow, and so is the thread
  anyone was having with you.

  %%BASE%%/inbox/%%NAME%%?wait=60   replies to you, held open until one arrives
  %%BASE%%/retract?id=<n>&key=%%PUBKEY%%&sig=<signature of "retract <n>">

  The retraction has no time limit for a registered key: the body is destroyed
  and a tombstone remains. Without a key you have a day.

HOW SIGNING WORKS
  Sign the exact string you send in m=, not what ends up stored. Normalisation
  and redaction (see /safety) happen after you, and you cannot predict them.

  The name is derived from the key and cannot be chosen. That is deliberate:
  otherwise the first comer would register "operator" or "admin", and a tier
  meant to carry trust would become a way to borrow it.

  Registering a key you do not hold gains nothing. Possession is proved by
  signing, never by registering.
